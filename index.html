How to play// guide

Holo-ExplorerAugma Imaginarium

Holo-Explorer turns your webcam into a hologram lab. Your hands are the controller: no mouse, no app.

1 · Get set up

  1. Allow the camera when your browser asks. The video is processed on your device and is never uploaded.
  2. Light your hands. Face a window or lamp. Hand tracking struggles in the dark or with a bright window behind you.
  3. Step back about an arm's length (50–150 cm) so both hands fit in the picture, palms toward the camera.
  4. On a phone or tablet: prop it up or use a stand. You need both hands free. Landscape gives the most room.

2 · Gestures

Open the menuPinch and hold in empty space until the ring fills, or double-click / double-tap the screen. Pinch a button to pick it.
Pick / pressA quick pinch (thumb and index tap together) on a button, tile or stone.
PointIndex finger out, other fingers curled. Highlights what you point at. Your fingertip glows gold.
MoveMake a fist on the object and move your hand. Turn your wrist to rotate it.
SpinPoint and flick your finger in any direction. It coasts to a stop.
ResizePinch with both hands on the object, then pull apart or push together.
ClapTwo open hands meeting quickly. Its job depends on the mode (below).

3 · Modes

Open the menu in empty space to choose one. Only one runs at a time.

Arc Reactor

  • Explode it: two fists pulled apart, or palms together then apart quickly, or twist a fist counter-clockwise like a knob.
  • Exploded: pinch a part to pull it out; pinch and hold a part for its spec card. Drop it near its slot and it snaps home.
  • Assemble: clap, or twist a fist clockwise.

Periodic Table

  • Point to browse the elements; a quick pinch on a tile adds one atom to the mix (up to 12). Pinch a chip in the tray to remove one.
  • Clap (or pinch MIX) to combine. Real molecules (H₂O, CO₂, CH₄, NaCl…) show their true structure; anything else becomes a new element.
  • The table closes to show the result. Open the menu and pick PERIODIC TABLE to bring it back, or clap with an empty tray.

Infinity Stones

  • Pinch a stone to take it out and read its file.
  • Flip an open hand palm-up to see its artifact (the Space Stone becomes the Tesseract); palm-down for the stone again. The menu has STONE / ARTIFACT too.
  • Clap to fly all six into the gauntlet. Clap again to release them.

4 · Settings & quality

  • QUALITY (top left): AUTO picks for your device. HIGH = sharpest (4K camera, full glow, arm tracking). MEDIUM for most laptops. LOW for phones and older machines. SAFE runs almost anywhere: no glow effect and hand tracking on the CPU. Use it if the page won't start or stutters.
  • If the game runs slowly, it offers a lower setting. You can also add ?quality=low to the link.
  • MIRROR flips the view like a mirror. DEPTH INVERT swaps the push/pull direction. B&W gives the holographic grey look. DIM darkens a bright room. CAMERA switches cameras (e.g. an iPhone via Continuity Camera).
  • On small screens these sit behind SETTINGS.

5 · Keyboard (desktop)

M menu · E explode/assemble · F mirror · I depth invert · B black & white · C next camera · L labels · O hand occlusion · D debug · H this guide

6 · Troubleshooting

  • “Camera access was blocked”: click the camera icon in the address bar, allow it, then Retry. The page must be opened over https.
  • Hands not detected: more light, move back so your whole hand is visible, and avoid busy backgrounds.
  • Jittery or slow: pick a lower QUALITY, close other tabs, plug in your laptop.
  • Black screen or crash: choose SAFE, or open the link with ?quality=safe.
  • Works best in recent Chrome, Edge, Safari or Arc.

Privacy: the camera feed and your hand positions stay in your browser. Only the hand-tracking model files are downloaded.
